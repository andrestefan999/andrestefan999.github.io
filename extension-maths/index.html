Why should I study Mathematics Extension 2?

Extension 2 dives into advanced abstract concepts like complex numbers, mechanics, and advanced calculus. It provides exceptional ATAR scaling, builds vital problem-solving foundations for university engineering, physics, and mathematics degrees, and makes first-year university math significantly easier.

How does the Mathematics Extension course structure work?

Advanced math no longer counts, instead, Extension 1 and Extension 2 are both worth 2 units each, giving you 4-units of mathematics block that provides exceptional ATAR scaling for engineering and science degrees.

What's the best way to study Extension 1 & 2?

Rote learning won't work for extension maths. Focus heavily on doing a wide variety of past trial papers, maintaining an error log for tricky calculus or proof steps, and practicing active recall under strict time conditions.

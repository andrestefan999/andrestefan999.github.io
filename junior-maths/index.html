What junior years do you teach?

I provide dedicated tutoring for students across Year 7, Year 8, Year 9, and Year 10, helping them build strong fundamentals before senior school. I do not cover primary school maths.

How do online Zoom lessons work?

We use an engaging, interactive digital whiteboard where students can write, solve equations, and visualize geometry concepts side-by-side with me in real time.

How does junior tutoring prepare students for senior high school?

By solidifying core algebraic manipulation, fraction work, and problem-solving habits early, students avoid common gaps that make Year 11 and 12 mathematics stressful.
